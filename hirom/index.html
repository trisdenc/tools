HiROM Studio

Hardware-accurate tools for making SNES music and games, right in your browser.

Apps

HiROM Sound

Compose music and sound effects that sound like the real S-DSP sound chip, not a generic chiptune.

  • 8-voice S-DSP engine, bit-exact Gaussian interpolation
  • BRR sample studio with loop fitting and A/B audition
  • Piano roll and tracker on the same song
  • ADSR/GAIN envelopes, pitch modulation and noise
  • Echo with an 8-tap FIR filter editor
  • SFX bank that ducks music voices like real games
  • Live 64 KB audio RAM budget
  • WAV export with loop points, stems and SFX packs

HiROM Pixel

Draw sprites, tiles and whole scenes inside the real limits of the S-PPU graphics chip.

  • True indexed color with 15-bit BGR555 CGRAM
  • 16 × 16 sub-palettes, 2/4/8bpp art
  • Pixel-perfect pencil, shapes, fill and dither brushes
  • Tilesets and BG1–BG3 tilemaps
  • Scene composer with main/sub screens, color math and parallax
  • Animated sprites and palette cycling at 60 Hz
  • Live 64 KB VRAM budget
  • PNG, indexed PNG, .pal, spritesheet and Tiled map export
